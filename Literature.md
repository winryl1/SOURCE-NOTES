**Literature** is a body of written works **associated with creative works of poetry.** It can either be in **oral or written/text** form. 

> [!example] Characteristics
> 1. Narrates a story
> 2. Expresses feelings, thoughts, and ideas 
> 3. Delivers significant information/lesson and crosses boundaries

## Genres/Major Forms 
### Poetry
It is characterized by having element of **rhythm, sound, imagery, and form.** It can be divided into: 
1. **Narrative:** focuses on storytelling; has the elements of a narrative
2. **Dramatic:** emotionally appealing; also tells a story and is intended to be recited or sung 
3. **Lyric:** focuses on expressing feelings rather than storytelling.
