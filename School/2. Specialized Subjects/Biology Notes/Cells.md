Living organisms have **two main types:**
1. **Prokaryotes:** unicellular with no nucleus 
2. **Eukaryotes:** multicellular with a nucleus

 Cells also have to be **smaller** to allow for **higher SA:V ratio,** which increases efficiency in exchanging materials. 

# Major Parts 
1. Cell Membrane: controls what enters and exits the cell (selectively permeable)
2. Cytoplasm: jelly-like fluid that fills the cell and holds organelles in place
3. Nucleus: control center of the cell; stores DNA and controls cell activities 
4. Nuclear Membrane: protects the nucleus; also selectively permeable 
5. Endoplasmic Reticulum
	- Smooth: no ribosomes; makes lipids and detoxifies harmful substances
	- Rough: has ribosomes; makes and transports proteins
