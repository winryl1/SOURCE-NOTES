# Prokaryotic Cell 
## Binary Fission
It is an **asexual reproduction** that is performed by separating the body into two new bodies. It is very similar to [[#Mitosis]] but has a different purpose. While Mitosis focuses on growth and replacing old cells, binary fission is how bacteria increases its population. 
### Steps 
1. **DNA Replication Initiation**: The DNA molecule is replicated on the **origin of replication** 
2. **Duplication & Segregation**: The replicated molecules move to opposite ends of the cell
3. **Cell Growth**: Bacterium grows in size, elongating and preparing a division site at mid-cell
4. **Septum Formation:** A protein complex **marks the division site** and guides new cell wall formation
5. **Cytokinesis**: Septum is formed and divides the cell's cytoplasm and forming two separate daughter cells.
## Spores 
A spore is a special kind of reproductive cell that develops into a new organism. Spores facilitate the effective development of genetically varied offspring, increasing their ability to adjust to environmental conditions.
