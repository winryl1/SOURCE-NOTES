# Endosymbiotic Theory
According to the **endosymbiotic theory**, the mitochondrion and chloroplasts were once prokaryotes who were engulfed by larger cells. Over time, they **created a symbiotic relationship**, benefitting off of each other.  This explains why mitochondria and chloroplasts are **similar to prokaryotes**.
## Evidence
1. Has a separate DNA (circular)
2. Has 70S ribosomes 
3. Reproduce via binary fission 
4. Have double membranes (consistent with engulfing)
