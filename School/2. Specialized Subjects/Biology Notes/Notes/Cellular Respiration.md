# Stages 
## Glycolysis
- Occurs in Cytoplasm
- Aerobic process (requires oxygen) and requires 2 ATP 
- Splits Glucose --> 2 Pyruvate
	- **Net Gain:** 2 ATP, 2 NADH
	- Pyruvate (3-Carbon molecule)
	- Glucose (6-Carbon molecule)
## Pyruvate Oxidation (preparation step for Krebs Cycle)
- Occurs in mitochondrial matrix
- Aerobic process (requires oxygen)
- Converts 2 Pyruvate --> 2 Acetyl-CoA 
	- Acetyl-CoA (2-Carbon)
	- also releases **2 NADH**
## Krebs Cycle/Citric Acid Cycle
- Aerobic process (has oxygen)
- Results in (2 pyruvates from glucose):
	- 6 NADH
	- 2 FADH2
	- 2 GTP/ATP
	- 4 CO<sub>2</sub>

**Steps:**
1. Acetyl-CoA + oxaloacetate = **Citrate** 
2. **Isomerization:** Citrate→ Isocitrate (converted by Aconitase)
3. Isocitrate → a-ketoglutarate (oxidized by Isocitrate dehydrogenase); releases CO<sub>2</sub>; releases energy (electrons) to reduce NAD<sup>+</sup> → NADH
4. a-ketoglutarate → Succinyl-CoA (oxidized by a-ketoglutarate dehydrogenase);  releases CO<sub>2</sub>; releases energy (electrons) to reduce NAD<sup>+</sup> → NADH
5. Succinyl-CoA → Succinate (converted by Succinyl-CoA synthase); creates GTP
6. Succinate → fumarate (oxidized by Succinate dehydrogenase); produces QH2 which converts FAD → FADH2
7. Fumarate → Malate (converted by fumarase) 
8. Malate → oxaloacetate (oxidized by Malate dehydrogenase); releases energy (electrons) to reduce NAD<sup>+</sup> → NADH; cycle restarts 
## Oxidative phosphorylation
- Uses FADH2 & NADH to generate ATP 
- Aerobic process (requires oxygen)
- **Equation:** NADH + FADH2 + O2 + ADP + Pi → NAD+ + FAD + H2O + ATP
- **Net Gain:** 
	- 6 NADH produces 15 ATP (2.5 ATP per NADH)
	- 2 FADH2 produces 3 ATP (1.5 ATP per FADH2)
**Steps:**
1. NADH and FADH2 donate their electrons to complex I and complex II, respectively, and turning them into NAD<sup>+</sup> and FAD.
2. Electrons are passed to ubiquinone (Q) through ETC. This releases energy and [[Photosynthesis#^Chemiosis]] occurs
3. Electrons from Q are passed to complex III through ETC, and [[Photosynthesis#^Chemiosis]] occurs. 
4. It then goes to cyt C, which then delivers to complex IV, and [[Photosynthesis#^Chemiosis]] occurs.
5. At the end of ETC, electrons are passed to O<sub>2</sub>, which splits into 2 oxygen atoms and takes up H<sup>+</sup> to form water. (4 electrons required to reduce 1 O<sub>2</sub>; 2 water molecules are produced)
6. The proton gradients created then undergo [[Photosynthesis#^photophosphorylation]].
