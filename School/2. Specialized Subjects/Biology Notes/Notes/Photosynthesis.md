> [!success] Photosynthesis Overview
> The process that uses sunlight, along with the products of Cellular Respiration to create glucose and oxygen (Products). In short, it is the conversion of light energy to chemical energy. It has **two parts:**
> 1. Light-Dependent Reactions
> 2. Calvin Cycle

# Types of Light-Dependent Reaction
## Summary
| <center> Non Cyclic </center>                        | <center>  Cyclic </center>          |
| ---------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Photosystem II & I                                   | Photosystem I                       |
| Result: ATP & NADPH| Result: ATP only                    |
| Releases Oxygen due to Photolysis                    | No Oxygen due to lack of Photolysis |
| Electrons are not cycled (replaced using photolysis) | Electrons are cycled                |
## Non Cyclic Pathway (Z-Scheme) 
- Requires Photosystem(PS) II and I 
- Has 2 Reaction Center Chlorophyll (P680 & P700) 
![[Non-Cyclic|500]]
1. **Light Absorption:** PS II and I absorb light energy, which excites electrons and reach P680;
2. **ETC:** Excited electrons travel through Plastoquinone and Cytochrome b6f complex and into plastocyanin, losing energy. **Chemiosmosis** occurs, which pump protons (H+) across the thylakoid membrane ^Chemiosis
3. **Photolysis:** H<sub>2</sub>O molecules are split into O<sub>2</sub>, Hydrogen ions, and electrons. The electrons produced replaces the electrons lost in PSII. The O<sub>2</sub> released is byproduct of this step.
4. **Photophosphorylation:** occurs as protons flow into ATP synthase, it uses its energy to convert ADP (adenosine diphosphate) and phosphate (Pi) into ATP.  ^photophosphorylation
5. **Light Absorption:** Electrons arrive at PSI where they are re-energized again, which are then passed to P700 and into ferredoxin.
6. **NADPH Production:** These are then passed to NADP+ reductase, which converts NADP+ → NADPH with protons and from the stroma, and electrons.
## Cyclic Pathway
- Closed circular pathway route  
- Only have Photosystem (PS) I
- Reaction Center (P680)
![[Cyclic LR|500]]
1. **Light Absorption & Excitation:** PS I absorb light energy, which excites the electrons and reach P700;
2. **ETC**: Excited electrons are passed to Ferredoxin and into the chain. As the electrons pass through, it loses energy to pump protons (H+) across the thylakoid membrane  
3. **Photophosphorylation:** occurs as protons flow into ATP synthase, it uses its energy to convert ADP (adenosine diphosphate) and phosphate (Pi) into ATP.
4. **Electrons return to PS I** 
# Calvin Cycle
- Conversion of CO<sub>2</sub> to glucose 
- Contains ribulose biphosphate (RuBPs)

1. **Carbon Fixation:** CO<sub>2</sub> molecule combines with RuBP, making a 6-carbon molecule and breaks apart into two 3 PGAs. 
2. **Reduction**
	- Requires ATP & NADPH. One phosphate from ATP will be given to PGA → BPGA. The NADPH then gives an electron and reduces BGPA → G3P. 
	- **Byproduct:** NADP+, phosphate (Pi), ADP (recycled for light reactions)
	- **Required:** 2 G3Ps (for glucose)
	- 1 Cycle = 2 G3Ps (needs 3 cycles to create **1 usable** G3P)
	- Total Cycles: 6 cycles (**2 usable** G3Ps, 10 remaining G3Ps)
3. **Regeneration of RuBP:**  The remaining G3P molecules will be used to regenerate the RuBP molecule necessary for another cycle.
