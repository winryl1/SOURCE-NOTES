

> [!summary] 
> The **Endocrine System** is in charge of creating and monitoring hormones to maintain bodily functions. 

# Parts 
## CNS
1. Hypothalamus: Controls the function of the Pituitary gland 
	- Thyrotropin-Releasing Hormone (TRH): controls TSH
	- Gonadotropin-Releasing Hormone (GnRH): stimulates production of FSH and LH (see [[Reproductive System#Processes (Female)]])
	- Corticotropin-Releasing Hormone (CRH): initiates release of ACTH
2. Pituitary Gland (Anterior & Posterior): Holds and secretes hormones
	- Posterior
		- Oxytocin: causes the uterus to contract during childbirth; also controls milk production 
		- Antidiuretic: slows down urine production to preserve water (**vasopressin**)
	- Anterior
		- Growth Hormone (GH)
		- Follicle stimulating Hormone (FSH)
		- Prolactin (PRL): affects mammary glands and milk production
		- Thyroid-Stimulating Hormone (TSH)
		- Luteinizing Hormones (LH)
		- Adrenocortiocotropic Hormone (ACTH)
3. Pineal Gland
	- Melatonin: affects skin color and sleep cycle
	- Seratonin: influencing learning, memory, happiness as well as regulating body temperature, sleep, sexual behavior and hunger
## Torso to Throat 
1. Thyroid (Master of Hormonal Glands)
	- Thyroxine (T4) & Triiodothyronine (T3): plays a role in metabolic processes
	- Calcitonin: lowers calcium levels 
2. Parathyroid: releases Parathyroid Hormone (PTH); raises calcium level 
3. Thymus: 
	- Important for **T-cell maturity development**
	- Also signals creation of T-cells (Thymosin)
4. Adrenal Glands 
	- Medulla
		- Epinephrine (adrenaline) 
		- Norepinephrine (noradrenaline)
	- Cortex 
		- Glucocorticoids:
		- Mineralocorticoids: balances the sodium and potassium 
5. Pancreas: in charge of breaking down sugar and take in glucose (insulin); also increases glucose (glucagon)
## Gonads (Reproductive)
### Female
1. Estrogen: responsible for female characteristics 
2. Progesterone: helps other hormones, lactation, menstrual cycle/pregnancy
