**Cell theory** is one of the **basic principles of biology**. With the invention of the microscope, scientist were able to discover cells, which later resulted in the formation of cell theory. Cell Theory has 3 [[#Fundamental Tenets/Postulates]] 
# Fundamental Tenets/Postulates
1. Living organisms are made up of cells
	- Organisms can either be **unicellular** or **multicellular**
	- Contribution by: Matthias Schleiden & Theodor Schwann
2. The cell is the basic unit of life
	- For an organism to be considered living, **it must have a cell**
	- By looking inside a cell, we can see the **movement of organelles** which proves that **cells are indicators of life**
	- All life processes (metabolism,[[Cell Reproduction]], etc.) occur within cells; this is **crucial for survival**
	- Contribution by: Matthias Schleiden & Theodor Schwann
3. New Cells are created from pre-existing cells 
	 - The creation of new cells comes from the division of other cells (Mitosis & Meiosis)
	 - This principle was added by Rudolf Virchow
	 - Contribution by: Rudolf Virchow 
---
Living organisms have **two main types:** [[Prokaryotes and Eukaryotes]] 

Cells also have to be **smaller** to allow for **higher SA:V ratio,** which increases efficiency in exchanging materials.
