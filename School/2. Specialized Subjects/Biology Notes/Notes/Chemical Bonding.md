# Chemical Bonding 
- Process where two atoms combine to form a new product 
- You can use the periodic table to:
	- Periods: rows; shows how many shells there are in an atom 
	- Groups: column; shows how many valence electron an atom has
	- This does not apply to **Transition Metals, Lactinide, Actinide** 
- **Valence Electrons:** electrons at the outermost shell; responsible for the chemical properties of an atom
- **Octet rule:** When bonding with another atom, an atom wants to achieve stability (8 valence electrons)

# Types of Chemical Bonding 
## Ionic Bonding/Electrocovalent 
- Involves complete transfer of valence electrons from one atom to another 
- Generates two oppositely charged ions 
- Occurs between **Metals** and **Non-metals**
	- Cation: metal
	- Anion: non-metal
##
---
## Covalent Bonding/Molecular Bond
- Characterized by sharing of electrons between two atoms 
- Occurs only between **Non-metals**
- Responsible for the formation of Carbon dioxide
##
---
## Metallic Bonding
- Responsible for the formation of metal 
- Occurs between metal atoms; electrons are free to move throughout (delocalized) which causes its properties such as malleability and conductivity

# Electron Affinity and Electronegativity
- **Electron Affinity:** ability to accept one or more electrons from another atom 
- **Electronegativity:** ability of an atom to attract electrons from a molecule
