Modern work culture and lifestyle has caused inactive and unhealthy practices, with almost **90% of Filipino youth** lack physical activity. Experts suggest that teenagers must perform **moderate to vigorous** physical activities for at least one hour a day. Even then, Filipino teenagers considered **among the most sedentary** in the world (WHO, 2007).

# Physical vs Exercise 
**Physical fitness** is the ability of the body to work efficiently without feeling fatigue.

**Physical activities** refers to the body movement that is carried out by muscles and consumes energy. 

**Exercise** refers to the structured body movement along with conscious breathing, performed regularly for physical fitness. (See[[#Health-Related]] )

# Fitness Types 
## Health-Related
### Body Composition 
This refers to proportion of body fat and body mass. A healthy body has **less fat** and **more body mass**.
### Cardiovascular Endurance 
This is the ability of the respiratory system of the body to **work and supply oxygen efficiently** during a sustained activity or exercise.  
### Muscular Strength and Endurance 
Muscular strength is the ability of a particular muscle group to **exert maximal force in a single effort.**

Muscular endurance is the ability of the muscle to **sustain repeated muscle activity** without fatigue. 
### Flexibility
This is the **ability of the joints** to move through an unrestricted, pain-free range of motion.
