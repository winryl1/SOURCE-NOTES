# Regulatory 

> [!info]- Definition
Communication can be used to **regulate/restrict** the receiver's action. It can also be used to organize a community. 

> [!example]- Examples
> - Setting a curfew
> - Checking the attendance 
> - Road signs 
> - Telling someone to be quiet
# Social Interaction 
> [!info]- Definition
This is communication's **primary function**: to interact with other people and form connections. It is the dynamic exchange of messages.

> [!example]- Types
> 1. Exchange: doing something for a **reward**
> 2. Cooperation: acting together to achieve a **common goal**
> 3. Competition: **working against** another group to achieve a **common goal**
> 4. Conflict: clashing interests 
> 5. Accommodation: **middle ground** of cooperation and conflict; compromising/sacrificing to achieve cooperation and resolution
