There are different models of communication that explain different systems/processes of communication. There are **3 main types**:
# One-way
## Linear
A communication model that **does not require feedback** (passive receiver). This involves a sender giving out information that the receiver absorbs.

> [!example]- Examples
> - TV Report 
> - Public Speeches
> - Radio
# Two-way
## Interactive 
A communication model where the sender and receiver go "**back-and-forth**" with each other. This model involves a **feedback** (missing in linear). Tho feedback is present, it delayed comes after the initial message.
> [!example]- Examples
> - Email 
> - Text Messages 
> - Forums 
> - Q&A in classrooms 
## Transactional 
This model is similar to interactive but is **simultaneous**. This happens when communication is **ongoing and dynamic** (in other words, real-time). People can exchange messages at once, verbal or non-verbal. This also accounts for individual differences and contexts that influence the message. This model **best describes how we communicate with others**, especially people we are closest to. 
> [!example]- Examples
> - Video Call
> - Live and Casual conversation
