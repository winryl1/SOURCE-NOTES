# Intrapersonal Communication
## Types
1. **Internal discourse:** talking to yourself; within the mind 
2. **Solo-written**: written that is intended for oneself
3. **Solo-vocal**: the opposite of internal discourse; you are "thinking out loud"
	- Self Talk: referring to yourself as the second person
	- Soliloquy: speaking as first person
	- It is best to do this privately as you might be misinterpreted by other people
