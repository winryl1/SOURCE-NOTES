# Barriers 
Recognizing the different barriers in communication is important to avoid **communication breakdown** and turn you into a more effective communicator. 
1. **Language**: These happen when the participants do not have the same language, accent, or use unfamiliar words or expressions, jargon, and idioms (see [[Communication Noise#Semantic Noise]])
2. **Cultural**: See [[Communication Noise#Cultural Noise]] 
3. **Physical Barriers**: See [[Communication Noise#Physical Noise]]
4. **Prejudging**
	- This barrier is about **bias** and **preconceived notions**
	- It happens when a person believes he/she understands what the speaker means even before the message is completed. It happens when the receiver disregards the message as unimportant (see [[Communication Noise#Psychological Noise]]) 
5. **Lack of interest and attention**
	- This barrier is about **engagement** and **focus** 
	- Because the receiver's focus is shifted to something that's more interesting, the receiver is not interested in the person or his/her message (see [[Communication Noise#Psychological Noise]]) 
6. **Information overload**: This happens when the sender gives too much information that the receiver can take in a limited time (see [[Communication Noise#Psychological Noise]]) 
# Strategies 
## Avoiding Communication Breakdown 
1. Use time-gaining expressions to have more time to think 
2. Give positive remarks to show enthusiasm to the speaker 
3. Ask for repetition/clarification
4. Check your understanding 
5. Do not jump to conclusions 
## Communicate effectively
1. Be focused
2. Speak intelligibly (practice [[Concept of Communication#Paralanguage]])
3. Listen with your eyes and ears 
4. Minimize distractions
