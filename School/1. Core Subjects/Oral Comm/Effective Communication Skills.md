# Barriers 
Recognizing the different barriers in communication is important to avoid **communication breakdown** and turn you into a more effective communicator. 
1. **Language**: These happen when the participants do not have the same language, accent, or use unfamiliar words or expressions, jargon, and idioms. 
2. **Cultural**: See [[Communication Noise#Cultural Noise]] 
3. **Physical Barriers**: See [[Communication Noise#Physical Noise]]
4. **Prejudging**: This happens when a person believes he/she understands what the speaker means even before the message is completed. It happens when the receiver disregards the message as unimportant. 
5. **Lack of interest and attention**: This happens when the receiver is not interested in the person or his/her message.
6. **Information overload**: This happens when the sender gives too much information that the receiver can take in a limited time.
