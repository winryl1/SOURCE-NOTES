# Culture 
This refers to the system of knowledge shared by a people, and it is **created, learned, and shared** through communication. Without communication, culture cannot be preserved and transmitted.

**Self-awareness** is important to be able to communicate with others, especially with those who are different from you. This can help you relate better to others and show sensitivity to them. 
# Gender & Age 
## Gender 
This refers to the characteristics of men and women that are **socially constructed**. In general, men and women differ in terms of **communication style**. 
### Men-only conversations 
In this style, some tend to dominate the conversation. Men prefer to have personal space and do not talk about their personal lives, and usually have many topics they talk about. Men also like to compete with each other.
- **Main purpose**: To inform
### Women-only conversations 
In contrast, women are very open with their personal lives. Women are also typically more expressive with their emotions and can talk about one topic at length.
- **Main purpose**: To build/maintain relationship 
### Mixed conversations
In conversations with both men and women, the men usually initiate the interaction. But men are also more likely to interrupt the other person and women usually allow an interruption. Men also user fewer adjectives/intensifiers compared to women. 

## Age 
This refers to the time the person has been alive since his/her birthday. People of different ages/generations differ in communication style because of the changes in societal norms. 

# Social Status & Religion 
The foundation of intercultural communication, as far as social status and religion are concerned, is respect for each other's differences. 
## Social Status
This refers to a person's position in society. Social status can affect how people communicate with each other, as an individual is more likely to interact with others of the same social standing. There might also be a communication gap between a person of higher status and that of lower status. 
### Elements 
1. **Ascribed**: determined at birth through characteristics such as age, sex, race, culture, etc.
2. **Achieved**: acquired through ability, talent, skill, and perseverance.  

## Religion 
This refers to the belief in the existence of a supernatural or spiritual aspect of religion. Differences in religion is a common source of intercultural conflict which occurs when people of differing religions try to impose their beliefs on another.
