# Types 
## Intrapersonal
This refers to communicating with oneself through **introspection**. This only involves one participant; that is, the **individual** is **both sender and receiver**. 
### Types
1. **Internal Discourse**: involves "inner talk"/inside the mind
2. **Solo Written**: involves writing only intended for oneself
3. **Solo Vocal**: involves speaking to oneself out loud
### Aspect s
1. Self-concept
	- Attitude: one's way of thinking, feeling, or behaving toward something 
	- Beliefs: 
	- Values
	- All three reflect their behavior
	- All three must be consistent with one another 
2. Perception
	- Past experiences
	- Culture
	- Expectations
	- Emotions
	- Social Influences
	- Physical and Biological factors
	- These **affect our perception**
3. Expectation: sometimes called "mental pictures"
### Tips
1. **Engage in self-dialogue**: allows you to know yourself better
2. **Practice self-affirmation**: boosts confidence and self-esteem
## Interpersonal 
This refers to communicating between persons (see [[Concept of Communication]]) . 
### Types 
1. **Dyad**: composed of 2 participant
2. **Small group**: composed of 3-15 participants 
### Tips
1. Know the objective of the communication
2. Let others speak: don't hog the floor (in other words, keep talking while dismissing others)
3. Observe other participants' nonverbal behavior
4. Use appropriate language for the situation

## Public/Mass Communication
Public communication involves a single speaker and a sizable audience. The speaker is tasked to deliver a message or a speech of general interest to the audience. 
### Importance 
1. Inform: give information about a topic 
2. Persuade: convince people to a point-of-view or a course of action
3. Entertain: usually done in special occasions (bday, anniversary, wedding)
