# Mineral Properties 
1. Luster: quality and intensity of reflected light from the mineral
	- Metallic — opaque
	- Non-metallic — vitreous (glassy)
2. Hardness: measure of abrasion resistance 
3. Color & Streak: 
	- Color: color of the mineral 
	- Streak: color of the powdered mineral 
4. Crystal Form/Habit: external shape of a crystal or groups of crystals
5. Cleavage: breaks evenly
6. Fracture: opposite of cleavage, breaks into irregular and non-planar shapes 
7. Specific Gravity: ratio of the weight of a mineral to the weight of an equal volume of water
