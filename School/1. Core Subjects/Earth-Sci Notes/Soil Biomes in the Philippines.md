# Forest
Because of the abundance of plants, there is an increase of evapotranspiration, which results in excess water that percolates (filters/seeps) down the soil. 

**Dominant**: Alfisols and Spondosols (present in cool temperature climate, usually in NA & EU)

# Tropical 
**Dominant**: Oxisol (highly weathered tropical soil)

# Grassland 
They are renowned for being the most agriculturally productive soils in the planet.

**Dominant**: Mollisols (deep and fertile soils)
