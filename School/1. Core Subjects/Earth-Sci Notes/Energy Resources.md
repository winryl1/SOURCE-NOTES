# Non-renewable 
## Coal 
It uses coal to heat up water, turning it into pressurized steam. This pressurized steam can provide enough energy to move the turbine. The generator then generates electricity. 

# Renewable 
## Hydroelectricity 
It uses the mechanical energy of flowing water from a reservoir. The water travels through the pipe and goes into a turbine. The turbine spins and generates electricity. 

## Types 
There are different types of hydroelectric plants based on the reservoirs they use: 
1. Impoundment: Uses water from a dam 
2. Diversion: Uses water from river 
3. Pumped Storage: uses two water reservoirs
