The 21st Century Literature 

# Fan fiction
It is an inspiration of an original text that is uniquely different. However, one of the biggest issues with fan fiction is plagiarism/copyright infringement (uses original text without the permission of the author). It can also cause rumors that can ruin one's reputation. Therefore, fanfic writers use a pseudonym.

# Flash/Micro/Dagli Fiction 
It has approximately 1500 words or fewer. It is meant to be consumed in a very short period of time. It's a story goes straight to the point of the plot. 

# Meta Fiction 
These are fictional works about a fiction. author, narrator, main character directly speaks to the reader; reader plays a part in the story (breaks the 4th wall); a story within a story  

# Speculative Fiction
Works that imagine a world using what if?  (sci fi, fantasy, Narnia, Harry Potter)

# Utopian Fiction 
A world where everything is perfect (until something goes wrong) (truman)

# Dystopian Fiction
Imagines the world in chaos 

# Apocalyptic Fiction
Imagines the world ending 

# Post-Apocalypticc
Imagines the world after a world-ending event 

# Alternate History
Imagines a world where history is altered 

# Photographic Essay
Presents a narrative using images/scene — media
