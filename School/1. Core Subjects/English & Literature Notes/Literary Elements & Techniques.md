# Literary Elements 
## Characters
These are the **actors that play a role in a story.** There are different type of characters depending on the basis: 
### Based on Characterization 
1. **Flat**: Never changes (no development) throughout the story; only shows a limited range of personalities
2. **Rounded**: Personality develops throughout the story
### Based on 
1. **Protagonist:** the main character;
2. **Antagonist**: antithesis of the main character 

## Setting 
This refers to the **place, time, or period** that the story takes place in. 

## Conflicts 
This is the **challenges** that the main character faces throughout the story. This is also referred to as the heart of the story. Without conflict, there can be no story. There are **7 main types** of conflict: 
1. Man vs Man 
2. Man vs Self
3. Man vs Technology
4. Man vs Nature
5. Man vs Supernatural
6. Man vs Society
7. Man vs Fate

## Plot 
This refers to the sequence of events that occur in a story. There are **2 main types**: 
1. Linear
2. Non-linear 

## Point of View 
This is the **vantage point** of the speaker or the narrator.  There are **3 primary types**: 
1. **First Person**: uses self pronouns; the narrator is part of the story
2. Second Person: narrator tells you are part of the story 
3. Third Person: narrator is a spectator of the story (either **Objective** or **Omniscient**)
	- **Objective**: the narrator is at an entirely external viewpoint; only reveals observable behaviour **Omniscient**: the narrator is **all-knowing**; can access thoughts and feelings of all characters; can provide background information
	- **Limited**: the narrator's knowledge is limited to what the **main character knows**

## Theme 
It refers to the central messages that authors are trying to convey in the text.
