**Cell theory** is one of the basic principles of biology. It has 4 [[#Fundamental Tenets/Postulates]] 
# Fundamental Tenets/Postulates
1. The cell is the basic unit of life
	- For an organism to be considered living, **it must have a cell**
	- By looking inside a cell, we can see the **movement of organelles** which proves that **cells are indicators of life**
	- Organisms can either be **unicellular** or **multicellular**
2. Living organisms are made up of cells
	- All life processes (metabolism, growth, reproduction) occur within cells; this is **crucial for survival**
	- 1838: Matthias Jacob Schleiden established that cells were located in the small compartments of his plant specimens 
	- 1839: Theodore Schwann also discovered cells in animals
3. New Cells are created from pre-existing cells 
	 - The creation of new cells comes from the division of other cells (Mitosis & Meiosis)
	 - This principle was added by Rudolf Virchow
4. Cells carry genetic info in the form of DNA 
 
# Exceptions to Cell Theory 
- Viruses are not made of cells but rely on a host to reproduce 
- Mitochondria and Chloroplasts have their own DNA 
- Striated muscle cells and fungal hyphae have multiple nuclei
