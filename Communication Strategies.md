**Communication strategies** are actions undertaken to have a smooth flow of information or ideas among communicators. 

# Strategies 
## Nomination & Restriction
Nomination involves the **selection of participants** in a conversation

Restriction involves **limiting the participants** from contributing to the conversation. In other words, setting parameters to a conversation. 
### Factors
1. Social Relationships: the role of the participants
	- Higher authority: assigned to control the conversation
	- Lower authority: listeners and later become speakers when told to do so 
2. Environment: setting of the conversation
## Turn-taking
Process where a participant stops speaking and gives the floor for others to speak 
- Keep-turn
- Release-turn
- Take-turn
## Topic Shifting
Occurs when one intentionally or unintentionally changes the topic 
### Speaking Topically 
Concentrates on some phrases from the last statement mentioned by the speaker. The idea develops and sticks within the context 
### Speaking on the Topic 
Concentrates on a word, but the newly introduced idea may not be related to the context
## Topic Control
Occurs when the main or assigned speakers controls the conversation from shifting to another topic
## Repair 
This is the act of fixing violations in a conversation
## Termination 
The act of ending a conversation.
