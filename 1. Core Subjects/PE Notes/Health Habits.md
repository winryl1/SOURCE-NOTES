# Healthy Diet
## Core Concept 
Food is the **fuel and medicine** of the body. A balanced diet provides the essential nutrients needed for energy, growth, repair, and overall bodily function, directly impacting physical performance and health.
## Macronutrients & Micronutrients 
### Macronutrients 
These are needed in large quantities for energy and structure.

| Types        | Importance                                                           | Signs of Deficiency                                         |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Carbohydates | Primary source of energy                                             | Fatigue, dizziness, excess weight gain                      |
| Proteins     | Essential for building and repairing cells, tissues,<br>and muscles. | Muscle loss, weak hair/nails, impaired growth (Kwashiorkor) |
| Fats         | Store energy, absorb vitamins, support cell growth                   | Skin problems, weak immunity                                |
| Water        | Regulates temperature, lubricates joints, transports<br>nutrients    | Dehydration, headache, fatigue                              |
### Micronutrients 
These are needed in smaller quantities for regulating processes and preventing disease.

## Principles 
1. Adequacy: getting enough of each nutrient
2. Balance: not overconsuming one nutrient at the expense of another
3. Variety: eating different foods for a wide range of nutrients

## Poor Dietary Habits 
Poor dietary habits such as: 
1. consumption of unhealthy food and beverages
2. over/under consumption of food and beverage 
These can cause malnutrition, stress, fatigue, disturbed sleep cycle, major organ problems, and impaired function of brain and digestive system. 
### Eating Disorders
1. Anorexia Nervosa: self-starvation; fear of weight gain 
2. Bulimia Nervosa: Binge-eating followed by purging 
3. Binge Eating Disorder: Consuming large amounts of food without purging 
4. Pica: consuming substances that are not edible 
5. Rumination: regurgitation of consumed, chewed, and swallowed food.
6. Avoidant/food restrictive order: individual do not have an interest in eating 

## Eating For Exercise 
### Before 
Eat a carbohydrate-rich meal and moderate protein 3-4 hours before exercise. Eat also a small snack 1-2 hours before. 
### During 
Stay hydrated. For longer sessions exceeding an hour, consume 30-60g of carbs per hour. 
### After 
Eat within 30min-4 hours to replenish glycogen and repair muscles (carbs + protein)

# Unhealthy Habits 
Unhealthy habits are the primary drivers of preventable diseases. Replacing vices with heatlhy habits is crucial for long-term well-being. 
## Major Unhealthy habits
1. Smoking
	- A single cigarette has over 4,000 chemicals (nicotine, tar, carbon monoxide)
	- This can cause cancer 
2. Physical Inactivity
	- This can significantly increase the risk of NCDs
	- Inactivity along with [[#Poor Dietary Habits]] can lead to obesity
3. Excessive Alcohol Consumption
	- Leads to liver damage, cancers, mental health issues and sleep disorders
4. Lack of Rest and Sleep
	- Impairs tissue repair, cognitive function, and overall performance
	- 7-9 hours is recommended 
## NCDs related to Unhealthy Habits 

### Cardiovascular Diseases
These are diseases that **affect the cardiovascular system**. In the Philippines, majority of the people suffer from this. 
### Cancer
This is the **uncontrolled growth of the cell**. These cells do not undergo apoptosis (programmed cell death). It can either be 
1. Benign: do not have the potential to spread/metastasize
2. Malignant: has the potential to spread/metastasize 
#### Types 
1. Carcinoma: cancer in [[Animal & Plant Cells#Epithelial Tissue]] 
2. Sarcoma: cancer in [[Animal & Plant Cells#Connective Tissue]] 
3. Lymphoma: cancer in lymphatic system 
4. Leukemia: cancer of the blood cells; begins in bone marrow
### Diabetes 
A condition where the **blood sugar level rises**. There is **no cure** to diabetes but can be managed through healthy diet and lifestyle. 
#### Types 
1. Type 1: caused by low levels of insulin due to the immune system attacking the beta cells in the pancreas that produces insulin
2. Type 2: become resistant to insulin.
3. Gestational: occurs in pregnant women; their cells become resistant to insulin but disappears after childbirth. But women who have this are at a higher risk of type 2
### Chronic Respiratory Disease
These are diseases that **affect the lungs and air passages**. These can occur due to exposure to environmental pollution (i.e. tobacco smoke). This can prove to be fatal for individuals.

# Stress Management 
Stress is a person's response to unfavorable external and environmental challenges. This can cause strain and pressure on the individuals, which leads to a response physically and mentally. 
## Types 
### By positive or negative
1. Eustress: "positive" stress (short period of time); causes feelings of motivation
2. Distress: "negative" stress (short or long period of time); causes demotivation 
## Stages 
1. Alarm
2. Resistance
3. Exhaustion
## Coping and Management Strategies 
### Management
1. Manage Stressors 
2. Rest and Sleep 
3. [[Exercise and Fitness]] 
4. Use Relaxation methods 
	- Meditation
	- Toga
	- Deep breathing 
	- Visualization
	- Massage
### Coping 
According to WHO, coping is the ability to react, recover and overcome somethin stressful. 
#### Types 
1. Adaptive: a type of coping that confronts problems/stress directly
2. Instrumental: addresses the problem after recognizing
3. Emotion-focused: reducing and blocking negative thoughts and emotions
