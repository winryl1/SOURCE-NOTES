**Condensation** refers to the reaction that joins **small** biological molecules by removing $\ce{H_{2}O}$ (-OH from one reactant and -H from the other) while forming a new covalent bond between **monomers.**^condensation

**Hydrolysis** refers to the **reverse** process of condensation by adding $\ce{H_{2}O}$ to **break down** a larger molecule into smaller ones.^Hydrolysis ^Hydrolysis
