# Part 1: Thus Shall My Church Be Called 
In D&C 115, Jesus Christ declared that His Church will be called "The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints" (verse 3). By having this name, Jesus Christ is telling the world that **He is at the helm of this Church** and that it's the only true one. 
> [!NOTE] "The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints"
The — establishes the unique position of the Church among the other religions 
Church of Jesus Christ — establishes that this is His true Church
Of Latter-Day Saints — establishes that this is the same Church Christ established back in His time

**Omitting His name** from His Church means we are **shifting our focus from Him**. Being member of the Church means taking upon His name, that mean we have to **clearly acknowledge** Him. 

---
A "standard" is a rule of measure by which one determine exactness or perfection. By being "a standard for the nations" (verse 5) means that as disciples of Christ, we are to be a standard of holiness for the world to see. Like as Christ has said, "Love one another... by this shall all men know that ye are my disciples" (John 13:35). 
# Part 2: Law of Tithing 
In D&C 119:1-4, the Savior commands us to pay one-tenth of our income as tithing to the Church. The tithes we pay are used for the Church's operation. By tithing, we are showing the Lord that we are willing to sacrifice our worldly pleasures to obey His commandments. 
> [!info] FSY
> "As you fast and pay tithes and offerings, you show God that His work is more important to you than material things."

---
Some people think they have to pay a lot of money for their tithe, but the Lord does not care. Heavenly Father will love you for it. The only thing that matters to Him is that you **pay it with your heart**. Tithing is not a matter of money but **a matter of faith**. Just like Christ has said to his disciples:
> [!quote] Mark 12:41-44
> "Verily I say unto you, That his poor widow cast more in, than all they which have cast into the treasury: 
> 
> For all they did cast in of their abundance; she of her want did cast in **ALL that she had, even ALL her living**"
