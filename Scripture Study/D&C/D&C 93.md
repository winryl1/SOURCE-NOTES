# Part 1: Knowledge of Christ 
To grow in knowledge of Christ, we must first forsake our sins and come to the Father. (D&C 93:1). The Lord is the source of ALL truth, knowledge, and light. 

In verse 12, we learn from John the Baptist that the Savior did not have the fulness at first, but received it grace by grace from the Father. This is essential knowledge to better understand our divine nature. If Jesus Christ was born with no veil drawn over his memory, then His mission and victory are not real. If there was no veil of forgetfulness, then anyone can be perfect, therefore diminishing Christ's perfect life. The Atonement could only be infinite if the test was real—if the possibility of failure was real. So, it was necessary for Jesus to receive grace by grace **just like us.** 

This is what was meant in D&C 88:6 that Jesus "descended below all things". In order for Him to fulfill the Atonement, he had to experience ALL the challenges of mortality—including the veil of forgetfulness. This validates that His test was real. His victory was real. Not only that, but because of this, Jesus is able to succor and be our advocate, because he knows "the weakness of man and how to succor them who are tempted" (D&C 62:1). He has been there, He knows what its like. 

It is also revealed that **through Jesus** (the firstborn), the Father created "worlds without number... for mine own purpose" (Moses 1:32-33). 

Jesus is also the light and life of men (see [[D&C 88#The Light of Christ]]) 
# Part 2: Savior's Light and Truth 
The Lord then taught us "how to worship, and know what you worship... and in due time receive of his fulness." (D&C 93:19). To emulate him is the highest worship we can offer him. Jesus Christ obeyed his Father and grew by degrees of glory. He then declares that we have the similar potential for growth and godliness (v.20). 

In the previous part, we see descriptions of Christ. These descriptions are to increase our understanding of who God the Father and Son is and how they relate to each other.
