# Part 1: The Gathering of Israel 
In section 86, the Savior recounted his parable of the wheat and tares from the Book of Matthew.  And though the Savior has interpreted most of the details, some were left unexplained, namely: 
1. The men sleeping 
2. Wheat and Tares allowed to grow together 
3. Wheat being uprooted in an attempt to weed out the tares 
4. Wheat springing forth and starting to bear fruit 
---
## The Men Sleeping 
Though the original interpretation of the man sowing was Jesus Himself, in D&C, it says that its the apostles. There is no contradiction. Christ is the author of our salvation and he has instructed his apostles to go to the world and preach his gospel. Jesus Christ is the **ultimate sower**; the Apostles are the **operational sowers**. When the men "slept", it meant that they have died. 

After they have slept (died), the enemy (evil/devil's power over the world) has caused a Great Apostasy—a complete falling away from the original Church of Jesus Christ, and with it the priesthood was lost. 
## Tares 
The tares represent the evil doctrines and those who spread them. They are servants of Satan and are a source of temptation to the faithful. 
## Tenderness of the Wheats 
The wheat refers to the righteous who strive to live righteously. At first, the tares look nearly identical to wheat during the early stages of growth. It's not until maturity do they become distinguishable. This is why the Lord said (D&C 86:6): 

> "pluck not up the tares while the blade is yet tender (for verily your faith is weak), lest you destroy the wheat also."

This verse mirrors God's patience for us. He is **not quick to condemn** us but gives us time to for growth until distinctions become clear. God knows that we are still learning and developing our faith, and He **gives us time** to do so. 
## The Gathering 
After the time has passed, the Lord will gather and separate the wheat from the tares. Those who have accepted Him and kept his commandments will be gathered with the wheats and inherit the kingdom of the Father. And those who have not will be gathered with the tares. 

# Part 2: Prophecy of War 
In this section, the Lord revealed to Joseph Smith that there will be a war at South Carolina, and cause the "death and misery of many souls" (D&C 87:1). This section says that the Lord can reveal future events to us through His prophets. Jesus Christ is the Alpha and Omega. The beginning and the end. He knows all things (D&C 38:1-2). 

Though Joseph did not live to see the fulfillment of His prophecy, a portion of it was fulfilled nearly 30 years after. 

Furthermore, the Lord tells us to "stand in holy places and be not moved" (D&C 87:8). We are living in the last days, and we must prepare for the Lord's coming. The second coming is like a thief at the night. We do not know when the thief will come. Same as for the Savior, no man knoweth the hour or day of His coming. 

So, while we wait, the Lord tells us to stand in Holy places.
