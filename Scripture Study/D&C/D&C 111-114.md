# Part 1: All Things for your good
In 1836, the Church had financial problems. Joseph Smith, Hyrum Smith, Oliver Cowdery, and Sidney Rigdon went to Salem in hopes of a "treasure" that could help them with their problem. Though the Lord said was not displeased with their journey that may have been  rooted from good intent, he still points out their follies, or, lack of good judgement and faith.

This is an important thing to keep in mind. Like what Dallin H. Oaks has said: 
> [!quote] 
> "To avoid all possibility of error is to avoid all possibility of growth. ... If we are willing to be corrected for our mistakes, ... innocent mistakes can be a source of growth and progress."

God then tells them that he will "deal mercifully with Zion" (verse 6) and that the Saints don't need to worry because He will provide. Not only that, He also advised them to act wisely, but remain free from sin.
