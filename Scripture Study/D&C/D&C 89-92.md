# Part 1: Word of Wisdom
The **word of wisdom** is a commandment by the Lord that teaches us to **practice healthy living**. The Lord also teaches us to also avoid substances that impair judgment or are harmful or highly addictive, legal or illegal. 

The Lord **prohibits** the following harmful substances: 
- Alcoholic Drink/Strong Drink (verse 5)
- Tobacco (verse 8)
- Tea and Coffee (verse 9)
---
The Lord revealed to us foods that are good for our body: 
- Fruits and herbs (verse 10-11)
- Meat — but sparingly (verse 12-13)
- Grains (verse 14-17)

The Lord promises increased **health, wisdom, knowledge, and protection** to those who obey this (verses 18-21). 

---

**Principles**
1. God's law is for everyone (verse 3)
	- *"adapted to the capacity of the weak and the weakest of all saints, who are or can be called saints."*
	- God meets us where we are. God **doesn't make it impossible** for His children to follow Him and receive His blessings. 
2. Spiritual growth starts small 
	- Going back to verse 3, anyone can begin living this principle right away. Progress in **discipleship can start with small, simple, and consistent acts of obedience**, which then prepares us for greater responsibilities 
	- It also shows us that anyone has a place in God's plan **if they are willing to be so.**
3. Commandments are not burdens (verse 18-21)
	- *"walking in obedience to the commandments, shall receive health in their navel and marrow to their bones... find wisdom... and the angel... shall not slay them"*
	- Often times people find commandments to be burdensome because they "limit" what we can do. But we must remember that these commandments **comes from a place of love**, and following them enables our Father in Heaven to bless us with all that he has. 

# Part 2: The First Presidency
Jesus Christ leads His Church through the First Presidency and the priesthood keys he has given them. 

## Blessings 
1. They testify of Jesus Christ throughout the world (D&C 90:9), allowing all to hear of Him
2. They receive revelations on how to guide the Church and its members (D&C 90:14-16; 124:125-126)
3. They pass on priesthood keys to other members of the church (D&C 10:8-9)
4. They build up and regulate the Church, putting order (D&C 107:33)
5. God also says that along with the First Presidency, the Bishopric and High Council together handle important decisions for the Church while being directed by he Lord. This tells us that the Church is governed by revelation through councils, not unilateral authority (D&C 120)
