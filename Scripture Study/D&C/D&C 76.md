# Part 1: Eyes of Understanding
- [b] **Verses 1-10:** These verses tell us that God is a merciful and just God. 
- [I] Main Ideas: Those who follow Him righteously
	- *"Great shall be their reward and eternal shall be their glory"* (v.6)
	- *"To them I will reveal all mysteries, yea, all the hidden mysteries of my kingdom* (v.7)
	- The ultimate end of spiritual progression is not to only know that the revelations are true, but also to see visions and feel the Spirit and get the added light and knowledge.
- ["] Quote from Bruce R. McConkie: 
> "What a glorious dispensation we live in. ... the Lord desires to confirm his word in the hearts of **ALL WHO WILL HEED HIS VOICE**"

- [b] **Verses 11-19:** These verses tell us about **receiving revelation** 
- [I] Main Ideas
	- God rewards those seek Him with righteous intent 
	- Just like Joseph and Sidney, we have to prepare ourselves to receive revelation. 
	- We do these by reading and pondering the scriptures
- ["] Quote from Elder D. Todd Christofferson:
> "When I say 'study,' ... reading a few verses, stopping to ponder them, carefully reading the verses again, and as you think about what they mean, praying for understanding, asking questions in your mind, waiting for spiritual impressions, and writing down the impressions and insights...
> 
> ... you may not read a lot of chapters or verses in a half hour, but you will be giving place in your heart for the word of God..."

# Part 2: Testimony of Christ
In section 76, we are given details about celestial, terrestrial, and telestial. In verse 75, 79, it says that the people who go here are "honorable men... who are not valiant in the testimony of Jesus". The Lord tells us that in order to attain the highest glory, we need to be valiant in our testimony of Jesus Christ. 
> [!quote] Quote by: Quentin L. Cook
> To be valiant, we need to focus on the power of Jesus Christ and His atoning sacrifice to overcome death and, through our repentance, to cleanse us from sin, and we need to follow the doctrine of Christ. We also need the light and knowledge of the Savior’s life and teachings to guide us on the covenant pathway, including the sacred ordinances of the temple. We must be steadfast in Christ, feast upon His word, and endure to the end
