# Part 1: For our Good 
At this point, the Saints were being terrorized by violent mobs. The Savior provided comfort and counsel to the suffering Saints and instructed Church leaders how to handle their situation. The Lord counsels them to "rejoice evermore, and in everything give thanks" (D&C 98:1). He tells the Saints to give thanks for what they have and for the many blessings, and to despair a little less about what they may not have or what anxiety they may have during difficult times. He wants us to **exercise our confidence in Him**, and that **He will bless us** as a people because **he has always blessed us** as a people. 

In verse 2, we learn about "waiting on the Lord". As we continue to wait for the Lord's promises to be fulfilled, we must **continue to obey Him and to come closer to Him**. Serving as Jesus served increases our hope in Him. 
# Part 2: Filled with Compassion 
The Savior tells us that suffering can come from our own transgressions, poor choices of others, and as a natural part of life. The Lord can use these sufferings to chastise us and to sanctify us. But even if we are sinful, the Lord still extends His compassion for us.  The Savior perfectly understands the situation we are in and continues to work in our life.
