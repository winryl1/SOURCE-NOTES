
# The Light of Christ 
The Light of Christ is the governing principle in nature. It proceeds from God through Christ and gives light and life to all creation (D&C 88:6-13). It proceeds from God through Christ In its lower degrees, it is the "physical" light from the sun, moon, and stars. In its higher degrees, it is what gives us the ability to reason, discern, judge, and comprehend. 

One manifestation of this our **conscience**. The light of Christ is in all men (D&C 88:46). All men are born with the natural capacity to distinguish between right and wrong. And because of this, we are able to exercise our agency—which is **essential for our eternal progression.** 

However, there is a difference between the light of Christ and the Holy Ghost. The **Light of Christ** is omnipresent divine element that "**prepares us**" to receive the Holy Ghost. However, even if the light of Christ is present in all of us, its influence **can be overshadowed** when we continue to **work against it.** The Holy Ghost is **personage of Spirit.** And while the Holy Ghost is available to anyone, it is **not always present to everyone.** His presence is only felt through "obedience to the laws and ordinances of the Gospel" (A of F 1:3).
