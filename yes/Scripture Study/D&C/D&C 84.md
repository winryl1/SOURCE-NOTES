# Part 1: Priesthood and Ordinances
In mortality, priesthood is the power and authority of God given to men and has been passed and continued "in the church of God in **all generations**" (D&C 84:17).  

Without the priesthood and ordinances, the power of godliness cannot be made manifest unto men. Without them, people cannot receive all the blessings the Lord has in store for us. 
## Aaronic Priesthood 
The **Aaronic Priesthood** is the "*lesser priesthood*" of the two priesthoods. This holds the keys of the:
1. Ministering of angels: This is the authority enabling holders to facilitate the ministering of angels to worthy members, through ordinances and covenant-keeping
2. The preparatory gospel: This is the gospel of repentance, baptism, and the remission of sins, and the law of carnal commandments (D&C 84:27); it "prepares" us to receive the blessings of this "greater" priesthood

## Melchizedek Priesthood 
The **Melchizedek Priesthood** is the *"greater priesthood"*. This priesthood holds the key of the mysteries of the kingdom (D&C 84:19), allowing holders to have a deeper understanding of divine truths. They also carry the authority to teach and enact the **full gospel** of Jesus Christ.

# Part 2: Oath and Covenants of the Priesthood 
In the priesthood, God invites us to make covenants with him. As we covenant to be "faithful" and "magnify [our] callings", God promises that:
1. We will be "sanctified by the Spirit unto the renewing of their bodies" (D&C 84:33). 
	- To be made clean through the Holy Ghost and have its guidance 
2. We will become part of the "church and kingdom, and the elect of God" (D&C 84:34)
3. We will receive the "Father's kingdom; therefore all that my Father hath shall be given unto him" (D&C 84:38)

It is also impportant to note that God is the one who makes the initiates the oath, not the holders. God also cannot "break" His oath (D&C 88:40). 

---
However, if one breaks this covenant and apostatizes against the Church, he "shall not have forgiveness of sings in this world nor in the world to come" (D&C 84:41). 

According to Pres. Joseph Fielding Smith, this does not mean we will be come a son of perdition, but rather

> [!quote] 
> He will never again have the opportunity of exercising the priesthood and reaching exaltation. That is where his forgiveness ends. He will not again have the priesthood conferred upon him, because he has trampled it under his feet; but as far as other things are concerned, he may be forgiven.
