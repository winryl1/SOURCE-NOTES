> [!success] Protein Overview
> **Proteins** are made up of **amino acids**, which are essential macromolecules involved in various functions within living organisms. These amino acids **link via peptide bonds** to form:
> 1. Dipeptide: 2 amino acids
> 2. Polypeptide: 3+ amino acids

> [!example] Functions 
> 1. Act as [[Enzymes]]
> 2. Form Antibodies
> 3. Help in Transport
> 4. Structural Component
> 5. Hormonal Control
> 6. Muscular: 

# Structure 
There are **20** different amino acids that are commonly found in living organisms. They all have the same general structure: 
- Central/Alpha Carbon atom
- Amino group ($\ce{-NH_{2}}$)
- Carboxyl group (-COOH)
- Hydrogen atom (-H)
- R group: this is **variable** and determines an **amino acid's properties**

## Levels 

> [!summary] SUMMARY
> In biology, the shape of proteins define their function. At first, proteins start as a simple chain of amino acids that **fold into intricate shapes** that perform functions in your body. The structure of amino acids are broken down into **4 levels**
